# WHC Professional Certificate in Spa & Wellness Leadership

## Curriculum design - v1.0, 31 August 2026

The flagship WHC Academy qualification: approximately **24 hours of guided learning**
across 8 modules, for spa directors, senior managers stepping up, and hotel
leaders taking on wellness. Built to the WHC course standard (Spa Revenue
Fundamentals is the per-lesson template) and to accreditation-readiness
requirements. **No accreditation is claimed until formally granted.**

Positioning: this is not a technical beauty qualification and never competes
with CIDESCO-style training. It is the commercial and leadership education the
industry does not teach.

**Entry guidance (not a formal prerequisite):** 2+ years in spa/wellness
management or equivalent hospitality leadership. Spa Revenue Fundamentals
recommended first.

**Assessment model:** module knowledge checks (formative) + module assessments
(scenario and calculation, 80% pass, unlimited retakes) + workbook submissions
+ a final project. Written work is reviewed by WHC (manual assessment queue -
architecture exists via the certificate-review pattern; build when module 1
ships). Certificate issues only when all modules, workbook and final project
are complete.

**The workbook:** one document built progressively across all 8 modules.
Each module adds sections; the final project assembles them. The learner
finishes with a real 12-month commercial improvement plan, not a stack of
quiz scores.

## Module 1 - Strategy & Commercial Leadership (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** position the spa as a business unit; build the total-contribution
case (ADR premium, length-of-stay, booking-driver share); write a strategy as
explicit choices; manage strategic drift.

Lessons: The director's mandate · The value the P&L does not show · Speaking
owner: profit, risk, asset value · Strategy is what you say no to ·
Market analysis and competitive positioning.

Case study: the spa that was "only 5% of revenue".
Workbook: situation analysis of the learner's (or the fictional Harewood
Group's) property; strategy-on-a-page with named exclusions.
Downloads: total-contribution analysis template (xlsx), strategy-on-a-page (docx).
Visuals: contribution map diagram; strategy choices canvas.
Assessment: scenario set + written strategy critique (manual review).

## Module 2 - Finance: P&L, Budgeting & Forecasting (3.5 hours)

**Outcomes:** read a spa P&L line-by-line with benchmark ranges; build a
driver-based budget; run variance analysis in driver language; operate a
rolling forecast with ranges and triggers.

Lessons: Walking the P&L (worked monthly example) · Driver-based budgeting ·
Variance analysis · The rolling forecast · Payroll economics deep-dive.

Case study: revenue up six per cent, profit down.
Workbook: 12-month driver-based budget for the project spa.
Downloads: spa P&L model (xlsx), budget builder (xlsx), variance commentary
template (docx).
Visuals: P&L waterfall; budget driver tree.
Assessment: calculation set (budget from drivers; variance decomposition).

## Module 3 - Revenue Optimisation & Menu Engineering (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** run RevPATH management at director level; engineer the menu on
contribution per room-hour with strategic roles; design the rate architecture
(anchors, ladders, fences); build membership economics that fill troughs.

Lessons: RevPATH as an operating system · Menu engineering quadrants ·
Pricing architecture · The discount trap · Membership strategy and the
waterfall.

Case study: two years of 20% off, and the courage to stop.
Workbook: full menu engineering exercise + 12-month pricing calendar.
Downloads: menu engineering matrix (xlsx), membership economics model (xlsx),
RevPATH calculator (shared with Spa Revenue Fundamentals).
Visuals: menu quadrant matrix; membership economics diagram; daypart pricing map.
Assessment: calculation-heavy scenario set.

## Module 4 - People: Workforce Strategy, Succession & Compensation (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** design structure from the business model; run pay-progression-
productivity as one system; build succession depth and a skills matrix;
recruit in a structurally tight market; set compensation that retains.

Lessons: Organisational design · Total payroll strategy · Succession and
key-person risk · Winning talent · Compensation and commission design.

Case study: the resignation that redesigned the spa.
Workbook: org design + succession matrix + compensation review for the
project spa.
Downloads: skills matrix (xlsx), succession nine-box (xlsx), interview
scorecard (docx), compensation benchmarking sheet (xlsx).
Visuals: org structure options diagram; nine-box performance/potential matrix.
Assessment: scenario set + written workforce plan (manual review).

## Module 5 - Guest Experience & Quality Governance (2.5 hours)

**Outcomes:** govern quality through standards, audit and recovery systems
rather than personal inspection; run LQA/Forbes-calibre internal audits; use
complaint and review data as an improvement system; manage brand partnerships
(product houses) as strategic relationships.

Lessons: Quality as a governance system · The audit cycle · Service recovery
economics · Reading the guest-data estate · Product house partnerships.

Case study: the anniversary couple and the twelve-minute wait (director's cut:
building the system, not saving the guest).
Workbook: quality governance calendar + audit template for the project spa.
Downloads: internal audit workbook (xlsx), complaint log + root-cause tracker
(xlsx), partnership review agenda (docx).
Visuals: guest journey map with failure-point annotations; complaint decision tree.
Assessment: scenario set.

## Module 6 - Capex, Risk & the Boardroom (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** build a fundable capex case (TCO, return mechanism, payback,
downside, exit); price the do-nothing option; run a living risk register;
write decision memos; report to owners and ExCo with credibility.

Lessons: Anatomy of a capex case · Pricing deferral · The spa risk register ·
The decision memo · Owner and ExCo relationships.

Case study: the hydrotherapy pool that had to argue for its life.
Workbook: one full capex case + risk register for the project spa.
Downloads: capex model (xlsx), risk register (xlsx), decision memo template
(docx), board pack skeleton (pptx).
Visuals: payback/downside chart; risk heat map.
Assessment: written capex case (manual review) + scenario set.

## Module 7 - Pre-opening, Development & Design (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** run a pre-opening critical path; input commercially into design
and spatial planning (treatment mix vs thermal vs relaxation economics); plan
recruitment and training curves; set opening pricing and the first-year ramp;
handle snagging and supplier management.

Lessons: The critical path · Commercial input to design · The people ramp ·
Opening pricing and year-one targets · Procurement and supplier strategy.

Case study: the spa that opened beautiful and broke (fictional composite:
gorgeous build, no treatment-room economics).
Workbook: 9-month pre-opening critical path for a fictional new-build.
Downloads: critical path (xlsx), recruitment planner (xlsx), training schedule
(xlsx), snagging checklist (xlsx), equipment schedule (xlsx).
Visuals: critical path timeline; spatial economics diagram (revenue per m²
by zone).
Assessment: scenario set + critical path submission.

## Module 8 - ESG, Emerging Practice & the Director's Year (3 hours)

**Outcomes:** build an honest ESG position (energy, water, chemicals,
single-use, supply chain, team welfare) without greenwash; assess wellness
trends commercially rather than fashionably; structure the director's annual
rhythm (budget season, appraisals, menu prints, maintenance windows); prepare
the final project.

Lessons: ESG that survives scrutiny · Evaluating emerging wellness practice ·
The director's operating year · Presenting the plan (final project briefing).

Case study: the sustainability claim that unravelled - and the one that held.
Workbook: ESG action plan + the director's-year calendar.
Downloads: ESG self-assessment (xlsx), annual operating calendar (xlsx),
final project template (docx).
Visuals: the director's year wheel; trend evaluation funnel.
Assessment: scenario set + final project submission opens.

## Final project (target 4-6 hours within the 24, manual review)

**A 12-month commercial improvement plan for Harewood House Spa** (the
fictional 12-room property used throughout, with a full data pack supplied:
P&L, daypart grid, payroll, retail, membership, guest scores).

Required sections: situation analysis · capacity and utilisation · revenue and
pricing · payroll and productivity · retail · membership · people and
succession · guest experience · marketing and demand · KPIs and reporting
rhythm · prioritised action plan with quantified value, owners, milestones and
review dates.

Pass criteria: every recommendation traced to evidence in the data pack;
arithmetic correct; priorities justified by impact ÷ effort; the plan would
survive an owner's questioning. Reviewed by WHC with written feedback either way.
