# WHC Concierge - Checkpoint 5

## Job-first matching

- Makes the job role and seniority the principal compatibility factor.
- Caps unrelated job families at 20%, so shared generic answers cannot turn a
  Spa Director-to-Receptionist comparison into a meaningful match.
- Keeps related career progression comparable, such as Spa Manager and Spa
  Director, while still reflecting the seniority difference.
- Applies an insurance hard stop only to treatment-delivery or relevant agency
  work when the employer explicitly requires it.
- Prevents an incorrectly selected insurance checkbox from rejecting
  leadership, management or reception candidates.

This remains a test checkpoint and is not approval to merge to production.
