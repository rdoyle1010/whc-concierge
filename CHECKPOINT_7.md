# WHC Concierge - Checkpoint 7

## Candidate-approved CV intelligence

- Reads text-based PDF and modern Word `.docx` CVs privately on the server.
- Suggests role level, stated years of experience, services, product houses,
  qualifications and industry systems using the platform's shared taxonomy.
- Never returns, stores or logs raw extracted CV text.
- Never silently edits a profile. Candidates must review, approve and then save
  suggestions before they can affect matching.
- Rejects unreadable scans and legacy `.doc` analysis with a clear explanation.
- Preserves existing profile selections when approved suggestions are added.

This remains a test checkpoint and is not approval to merge to production.
