# WHC Concierge - Checkpoint 6

## Advertised-title correction

- Uses the advertised job title as the primary role identity.
- Prevents a stale or conflicting hidden role field from overriding the job
  candidates can actually see.
- Recognises common variations such as Spa Director, Director of Spa, Head of
  Wellness and Wellness Director regardless of capitalisation or word order.
- Retains the structured role-level field as a fallback for untitled records.

This remains a test checkpoint and is not approval to merge to production.
